.issue-badge Shows the story label "Daily Ritual" in the feature header. If absolute positioning is removed, the label stays in normal flow inside the feature header. Uses absolute positioning inside the relatively positioned feature header so the label stays anchored in the top-right without pushing the title or intro out of place. The containing block is .feature-header because it is the nearest positioned ancestor. Check at smaller widths and zoom to make sure the badge does not overlap the title or intro. If it does, move it back into normal flow with a responsive rule. Test at 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom to make sure the badge stays readable and does not overlap the title or intro.
.story-guide Provides quick links to the main sections of the coffee story. Without sticky positioning, the guide stays in normal flow above the article content. Uses sticky positioning on wider screens so the section links stay available while reading without removing the guide from normal flow. The sticky element stays within the main grid area and is bounded by its containing block as the page scrolls. Below the 52rem breakpoint, sticky positioning is not applied, so the guide returns to normal flow. Test scrolling at desktop width to confirm the guide sticks and stops at its section boundary. Also test 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom.
.pull-quote Highlights a short quote from the coffee story so it stands out from the surrounding paragraphs. Without the enhanced desktop styling, the quote stays in normal flow between the paragraphs. On wider screens, it uses a negative inline margin for a small controlled overlap and relative positioning with a raised layer so it stays above nearby content. The pull quote remains in the article flow. Its relative positioning and z-index create a raised stacking context using the layer token. Below the 52rem breakpoint, the overlap is removed and the quote stays in normal flow with its regular margins. Test the overlap at desktop width, 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom. Confirm it does not cover text, links, or keyboard focus.
.feature-figure Frames the main feature image and caption as one visual group. Without the rounded treatment, the figure still stays in normal flow with its border, padding, image, and caption. Uses border-radius to soften the figure container and separate it visually from the surrounding article content. The figure stays in normal flow and does not need its own stacking context for the rounded treatment. If border-radius is unsupported or removed, the figure simply becomes rectangular and all content stays readable. Test at 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom to confirm the figure remains readable and does not overflow.
.feature-figure img Adds a small visual shape treatment to the feature image without clipping the caption or other important content. Without clip-path, the image stays rectangular inside the figure and remains fully visible. Uses clip-path with a mild polygon so the image has a more editorial look without becoming too dramatic. The image stays inside .feature-figure. The clip-path changes only the visible shape of the image and does not create a new layout relationship with the text. If clip-path is unsupported or turned off, the image returns to a normal rectangle and the figure still works. Disable clip-path and confirm the rectangular fallback. Also test 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom to make sure no important image content is cut off.
.shape-portrait Supports the editorial section with a visual image that the text can wrap around. Without the shape treatment, the image stays in normal flow and the text remains readable below or beside it. Uses a floated image with shape-outside and shape-margin so the paragraph wraps around a circular shape instead of a rectangular box. The portrait stays inside its article section. The float affects text wrapping within that section, while the section's flow-root keeps the floated content contained. If shape-outside is unsupported or disabled, the image can still remain floated and the text wraps around its rectangular box. Disable shape-outside and confirm the rectangular fallback. Also test 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom to make sure the text stays readable and the image does not crowd the content.
